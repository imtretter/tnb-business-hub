California Vehicle Code — Operator Requirements
Per California Vehicle Code (CVC §21115 et seq.), golf carts operated on any public road require a valid California driver's license. Operators must be at least 16 years of age to operate on public roads; however, Owner requires operators to be at least 18 years of age and Renter must be at least 21 years of age. All operators must comply with California traffic laws. Golf carts as defined under California law (under 1,300 lbs, max 15 mph) are restricted to private property and designated areas unless meeting street-legal LSV/NEV equipment standards. Renter assumes all responsibility for operating the cart in compliance with applicable California and local laws.

Golf Cart Rules & Regulations
  1. Renter must be a minimum of 21 years of age to rent. Operators must be at least 18 years of age with a valid driver's license in their possession.
  2. Use lights after sunset. Turn off cart and lights when not in use to prevent battery drain.
  3. No horseplay, racing, off-roading, or other misuse. Do not endanger passengers, pedestrians, or property.
  4. Obey all applicable traffic and road laws. Park on level ground; remove key when not in use.
  5. Maximum 1 person per seat. Back seat weight limit: 250 lbs. No passengers on footrests.
  6. Report any malfunctions immediately to TNB Golf Carts: (530) 794-8787.
  7. Alcohol is prohibited during cart operation.
  8. Gas carts: Premium or Non-Ethanol fuel only. Electric carts: do not overcharge; do not use charger outdoors.
  9. In the event of snow or ice accumulation, remove snow from canopy to prevent structural damage.
